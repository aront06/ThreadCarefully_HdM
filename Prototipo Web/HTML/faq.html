FAQ

Common questions about Thread Carefully, the swinging mechanics, release platforms, and demo availability.

Thread Carefully is a high-stakes, 2.5D physics-based climbing and swinging rage game. As a daring silkworm/climber, you spin and latch tensile silk threads onto swaying branches and treacherous canopy obstacles. One miscalculated leap sends you tumbling back down!

It embodies the core gameplay! Every climb is a tense ascent where skill and physics master each height. When you fall, there are no checkpoints—just lessons, a bit of rage, and the determination to scale higher on your next attempt.
