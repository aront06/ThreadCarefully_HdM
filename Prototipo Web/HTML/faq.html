FAQ

Common questions about Thread Carefully, the swinging mechanics, release platforms, and demo availability.

Thread Carefully is a high-stakes, 2.5D physics-based climbing and swinging rage game. As a daring silkworm/climber, you spin and latch tensile silk threads onto swaying branches and treacherous canopy obstacles. One miscalculated leap sends you tumbling back down!

It embodies the core gameplay loop! Every climb is a tense ascent where skill and physics master each height. When you fall, there are no checkpoints—just lessons, a bit of rage, and the determination to scale higher on your next attempt.

Yes! As indicated on our status monitor, Thread Carefully is built with full gamepad controller support and optimized specifically to run at a solid 60 FPS on Steam Deck and PC.

Absolutely. A standalone demo showcasing the first section of the jungle canopy and core silk-swing physics will be available on Steam ahead of the full release. Wishlist the game on Steam to be alerted immediately when it goes live!

System Requirements

Minimum

  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i3-6100 / AMD Ryzen 3 1200
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 960 / AMD Radeon R9 280
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 4 GB available space
  • Additional Notes: Gamepad controller recommended for optimal swinging precision.

Recommended

  • OS: Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 (6GB) / AMD Radeon RX 580
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 4 GB SSD space
  • Additional Notes: 1080p / 60+ FPS target on High settings.
