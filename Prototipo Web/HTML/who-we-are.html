Who We Are?

We are a passionate team of student game creators at Hochschule der Medien (HdM) in Stuttgart, Germany. United by our love for challenging physics-based platformers and expressive hand-crafted art, we are building Thread Carefully as our graduation semester project.

LEAD & CODE

Game Design & Physics Programming

Mechanics, Level Pacing & Silk Line Physics

Architecting the core 2.5D swinging algorithms, pendulum gravity, camera physics, and high-tension level obstacle designs that test player patience and skill.

ENVIRONMENT ART

3D Environment Art & Shaders

Canopy Modeling, Foliage & Lighting

Crafting the rich 2.5D jungle treetops, textured bark, mossy vine shaders, and atmospheric backdrops that reward players as they ascend higher.

CHARACTERS

Character Rigging & Animation

Silkworm Mascot, Silk Thread & Rig

Bringing our daring silkworm and canopy inhabitants to life with expressive movement, responsive silk spinning, and fluid climbing animations.

SOUND & PROD

Audio Design & Production

Foley, Adaptive Music & Community

Composing the dynamic reactive soundtrack, recording realistic vine rustling and branch snaps, and connecting with our community across social media.

Get In Touch

Interested in playtesting or learning more about our student production at Hochschule der Medien? Reach out through our social media channels or drop us a message via our community Discord and YouTube!
