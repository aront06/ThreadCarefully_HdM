Legal & Privacy

Please read our guidelines regarding project intellectual property, telemetry, privacy standards, and website cookies for Thread Carefully.

1. Terms of Service

Thread Carefully is an independent videogame intellectual property developed by student creators at Hochschule der Medien (HdM), Stuttgart. All promotional materials, trailers, concept artwork, and code builds are protected under applicable intellectual property laws.

By downloading or participating in public playtests on Steam, you agree not to decompile, reverse-engineer, or redistribute demo binaries without explicit consent from the development team.

2. Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This promotional website does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) without your active consent.

When downloading our demo or participating in telemetry-enabled playtesting on Steam, anonymized crash logs, framerate benchmarks, and physics collision metrics may be collected exclusively for performance tuning and bug eradication.

3. Cookie Policy

This website utilizes minimal session-based local storage and standard technical cookies to preserve your navigation state (such as language preference and menu toggles). No third-party behavioral advertising cookies are embedded.
